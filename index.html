⭐ Team Formation & Eligibility

  • Department Restriction: Both players in a pair must belong to the same department.
  • Open Pairings: Teams can be Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles, or Mixed Doubles.
  • Unified Category: There are no separate gender categories. All registered pairs will compete against each other in a single tournament pool.
  • Skill Level & Category Choice: This game is only for Beginner and Advanced players. If a player is too good for their category, the organizers can disqualify the pair.
  • For Women Players: All women can choose to join the Beginner category. If they want to challenge themselves, they can choose to join the Advanced category. The choice is up to them.

⭐ Team Manager Rules & Responsibilities

  • One Point of Contact: Each department must have 1 Team Manager. Only the Manager can talk to the organizers about schedules, disputes, or player changes.
  • Submit Player Line-up: Before every match starts, the Manager must submit the names of the 2 Beginner pairs and 2 Advanced pairs who will play. You cannot change players once the match begins.
  • Player Eligibility: The Manager must make sure all their players are from the same department and are registered in the correct skill category (Beginner or Advanced).
  • Control Attendance: The Manager must make sure their players are at the court on time. If a pair is 10 minutes late, that specific game is a walkover (automatic loss).
  • Keep Team Discipline: Managers must ensure their players and supporters behave properly and respect the referee and opponents.

⭐ Match & Scoring

  • Set 1: Point 1 to 15
  • Set 2: Point 16 to 30
  • Change side when reaching 15 points
  • Pair who reach 30 points first will be the winner

⭐ Participation Limit

  • There is no limit on the number of players registered in a team, but only 2 Beginner pairs and 2 Advanced pairs may participate in each match.

⭐ Attendance

  • Any pair who does not arrive within 10 minutes of scheduled time will be disqualified.

⭐ Organizer Rights

  • Rules are subject to change.
  • Organizer decision is final.
